# Timber House Builder Website

## Goal
Create a complete, polished one-page website for a wooden house builder, inspired by the clarity and premium feel of the approved property site while remaining original and easy to rebuild in WordPress Elementor.

## What I’ll build
- Compact header with logo, section links, and consultation button
- Full-width timber-home photo opening with a strong builder message
- Trust figures and credentials
- Three featured wooden-home projects
- Builder story and craftsmanship section
- Simple four-step building process
- Service options for custom homes, cabins, and timber extensions
- Client testimonial and consultation prompt
- Contact form and compact footer
- Mobile-friendly layouts for every section

## Visual direction
- Warm natural palette: forest green, timber brown, soft ivory, and charcoal
- Premium serif headings paired with a clean sans-serif body font
- Large, realistic architectural photography focused on modern timber homes and joinery
- Restrained transitions and simple grids; no complex overlaps, masks, or effects

## Elementor-friendly approach
- Every area maps to standard Elementor containers, headings, images, buttons, counters, and form fields
- Conventional one-, two-, three-, and four-column structures
- Reusable spacing, colors, typography, and button styles
- No custom drawing, canvas, sliders, or difficult interactions

## Technical details
- Implement the complete design at the homepage path
- Generate and store all architectural imagery locally
- Define the visual system centrally with semantic design tokens
- Add complete page metadata and verify desktop and mobile rendering
